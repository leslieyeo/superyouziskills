### Route 1: Long-form Works / Interviews / Speeches

#### A-01 | First Principles Definition (Kevin Rose Interview, 2013)
> **Original**: "I think it is important to reason from first principles rather than by analogy. [With analogy] we are doing this because it is like something else that was done, or it is like what other people are doing. [With first principles] you boil things down to the most fundamental truths and then reason up from there."
> **Chinese translation**: 我认为从第一性原理推理而非类比推理很重要。[类比]是我们这样做是因为别人这样做过，或者因为其他人正在这样做。[第一性原理]你把事情归结为最基本的事实，然后从那里向上推理。
> **Source**: Kevin Rose interview (2013)
> **Cross-verified**: Business Insider, James Clear, CNBC, multiple independent sources

#### A-02 | First Principles = Physics Way of Thinking (Kevin Rose Interview, 2013)
> **Original**: "First principles is a kind of physics way of looking at the world. And what that really means is you boil things down to the most fundamental truths and then reason up from there."
> **Chinese translation**: 第一性原理是一种用物理学看世界的方式。它真正的意思是把事物归结为最基本的事实，然后从那里向上推理。
> **Source**: Kevin Rose interview
> **Note**: Same interview as A-01, complementary paragraph

#### A-03 | First Principles vs Analogy - Energy Cost Warning
> **Original**: "It takes a lot more mental energy."
> **Chinese translation**: 这需要更多的脑力。
> **Source**: Kevin Rose interview / YouTube (NV3sBlRgzTI)
> **Note**: Musk own acknowledgment of the cognitive cost of first-principles thinking

#### A-04 | Battery Cost First-Principles Analysis
> **Original**: "People would say, Historically it cost 600 dollars per kilowatt-hour, and so it is not going to be much better than that in the future. And you say, No, what are the batteries made of? First principles means you say, Okay, what are the material constituents of the batteries? Then you break that down on a material basis and say, If we bought that on the London Metal Exchange what would each of those things cost? It is like 80 dollars per kilowatt hour."
> **Chinese translation**: 人们会说历史上电池成本是每千瓦时600美元，所以未来也不会好多少。你要说不，电池是由什么构成的？第一性原理意味着你要问好吧，电池的材料成分是什么？然后在材料基础上拆解，说如果我们在伦敦金属交易所购买，这些东西各值多少钱？大约每千瓦时80美元。
> **Source**: CNBC (2018-04-18) / TED 2013
> **Note**: Classic cost curve quote - from 600/kWh pushed down to 80/kWh material floor

#### A-05 | Rocket Cost First-Principles Analysis - 2% Raw Materials Law
> **Original**: "What is the commodity price of the raw materials required to build a rocket? It turned out that the materials cost of a rocket was around 2% of the typical price."
> **Chinese translation**: 制造火箭所需的原材料大宗商品价格是多少？结果发现火箭的材料成本大约是其典型价格的2%。
> **Source**: TED 2013 / Harvard RCTOM case study
> **Note**: SpaceX cost curve core argument - raw materials only 2% of rocket price, rest is inefficient intermediaries

And what is the weight of the material that goes into the rocket?"

### Route 4: Third-Party Analysis / Criticism

#### C-01 | Walter Isaacson Biography Assessment (2023)
> **Core argument**: Musk is driven by an unchangeable internal essence - desire for control, obsession, resistance to rules, insensitivity, love of drama and urgency. These demons are both the engine of innovation and the source of controversy.
> **Criticism**: Vox argues Isaacson asked the wrong questions - too accepting of Musk own narrative, lacking critical analysis of geopolitical influence and X/Twitter acquisition.
> **Source**: Walter Isaacson "Elon Musk" (Simon & Schuster, 2023)

#### C-02 | Production Hell Academic Analysis
> **Paper**: "The production hell of Tesla: The theater of organizations and failed CEO performances" (ResearchGate, 2018)
> **Argument**: Model 3 production crisis was not just an engineering problem but also a failure of organizational reputation and CEO performance.
> **Source**: Academic research

#### C-03 | Atoms are Cheap, Process is Pricey - Methodology Analysis
> **Article**: Max Olson, Futureblind
> **Argument**: SpaceX success key is not Musk the person but specific methods, culture, and decisions. Question: Are these practices replicable?
> **Source**: futureblind.com
> **Note**: Correction to Musk personality cult narrative

#### C-04 | Vertical Integration Strategic Criticism
> **Argument**: Vertical integration provides cost advantage during supply chain stability but also increases capital intensity and single point of failure risk. Tesla success partly attributed to timing (traditional automakers EV lag).
> **Source**: Multiple business analyses (Business Insider, Reuters, CleanTechnica)
> **Note**: Giga Press ultra-large die-casting technology reportedly being abandoned by Tesla (Quartz, 2024) - methodology is also iterating

#### C-05 | Limitations of First Principles
> **Argument**: First principles works in hardware/physics domains but produces reductivist fallacies when applied to social science, politics, content moderation and other complex systems. Musk management controversy at X/Twitter partly stems from applying engineering thinking to social systems.
> **Source**: Vox, Washington Post, FT multiple media commentary
> **Note**: This is currently the most core criticism line

#### C-06 | The Algorithm Dark Side
> **Argument**: Musk question-delete-simplify-accelerate-automate process works in manufacturing but can be destructive in content moderation, employee management and other areas requiring nuanced judgment.
> **Source**: Multiple tech media coverage of Twitter/X layoffs and content policy changes
